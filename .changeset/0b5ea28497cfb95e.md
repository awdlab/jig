**Docs demos and app layout follow one naming scheme.** Every demo uses `jig-demo-{folder}-{file}` / `Demo_{Folder}_{File}`, cross-control demos move to `demos/_shared/`, `helper/` and `page-components/` fold into `utils/`, legacy `.component`/`.service` file suffixes are dropped, badge and stepper get playgrounds, scroll-shadow gets an A11y tab, and the unused `/test` route and `serve:ssr` script are removed. No published package changes.
