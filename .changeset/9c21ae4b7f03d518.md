**`<html lang>` belongs to the app again.** `I18n` started at a hardcoded `en` and wrote the active tag back to `document.documentElement.lang` on every switch, so a `<html lang="de">` app was silently relabelled `en` the moment a control was rendered — and the write-back could only be turned off by subclassing the service, because the underlying flag is `protected readonly`. The initial language is now read from `<html lang>` (falling back to `en` when the document declares none), and jig no longer writes the attribute. Two config options cover the rest: `language` sets the initial tag outright (`null` for the browser's preference), and `syncDocumentLang: true` restores the write-back for apps that do delegate their document language to jig.
