**The theme folders share one structure.** Shade and Material now ship a `global` part that extends the base one, like Nova. The themed `resizable` parts extend `base/resizable` instead of copying its CSS, so `resize: both` also applies in unstyled mode. `api/style`, `api/theme` and `api/utils` get `index.ts` barrels; `@awdlab/jig-themes/api` exports the same names as before.

Migration:

- `@awdlab/jig-themes/base/colors` is removed (it had no importers). Copy the palette you need into your own theme part.
